This paper presents a physics-constrained generative framework that pairs a conditional variational autoencoder with a risk-aware genetic algorithm for charging infrastructure planning, with equity treated as a feasibility constraint. The work is in scope and the framing is interesting, and there are a few areas that would strengthen the contribution. (1) the manuscript has spelling and consistency issues throughout, including repeated misspellings of technical terms and a duplicated step label in the introduction, and a careful proofread is needed. (2) the generative model loses substantial reconstruction fidelity once the physics penalties are activated, and the implications of that trade-off for the downstream planning results deserve a clearer discussion. (3) the counterfactual analysis relies on a small number of fixed seeds and a single benchmark topology, so broadening the evaluation and comparing against baseline planning approaches would help substantiate the claims.
